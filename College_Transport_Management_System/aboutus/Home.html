About College Transport Management System

About the project and its major facilities

About the System

The College Transport Management System is a web-based application developed to manage student transportation services. The system provides facilities for students to view available routes and buses, register for transport services, make payment records and view their registration details.

Administrators can manage students, drivers, buses, routes, trips, transport registrations and payments through a centralized system.

Student Facilities

  • Student registration and login
  • View available transport routes
  • View available buses and driver information
  • Register for transport services
  • Make transport payment records
  • View transport registration details
  • View payment receipts
  • Change student account password

Administrator Facilities

  • Manage student records
  • Manage driver records
  • Manage buses
  • Manage transport routes
  • Manage trips
  • Manage transport registrations
  • Manage payment records
  • View transport-related information and reports

Purpose of the System

The main purpose of the system is to provide a centralized platform for managing college transportation services. It helps organize student transport registrations, routes, buses, drivers, trips and payment information in a structured manner.
